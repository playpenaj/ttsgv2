📖 How Points Work

Think of points like a ranking score. Win matches to climb up. The better your opponent, the more you gain!

🎯 You start with: 1000 points
🏆 When you win: You gain points
😢 When you lose: You lose points

🌍 Inspired by ITTF

Our scoring system is inspired by the International Table Tennis Federation (ITTF) rating philosophy — where the amount of points you gain or lose depends on the strength of your opponent.

The ITTF uses an "Elo-like" system to keep competitions fair: beating a stronger player is rewarded far more than beating a weaker one. This encourages players to challenge up and keeps rankings meaningful.

Our implementation is a simplified version of that principle, tuned for community league play. It is not an official ITTF rating.

🧮 The Formula in Detail

Every time a match is approved, we calculate:

Rating Gap = |Loser Rating − Winner Rating|

Then we look up the base points using the table below. Finally:

New Winner Rating = Old Winner Rating + Base Points New Loser Rating = max(100, Old Loser Rating − Base Points)

For doubles: we use the average rating of each pair:

Team Rating = (Player 1 Rating + Player 2 Rating) ÷ 2

🎓 Points Made Simple

🎉 Upset Win (Beat someone stronger)
You gain LOTS of points (up to 40!)

😐 Expected Win (Beat someone weaker)
You gain only a FEW points (1–10)

😱 Bad Loss (Lose to someone weaker)
You lose a LOT of points

Example: If you (1000) beat a 1200-rated player, you might jump to 1030. But if you beat a 900-rated player, you might only go up to 1005.

✅ Both Players Must Confirm

No points are awarded until both players confirm the match result. This prevents cheating and disputes.
