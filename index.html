📖 Pointing System

This app uses a simplified version of the ITTF Rating Points System for singles and doubles.

How Points Are Calculated

1. Base Rating: Every player starts with 1000 points.

2. Match Result: When a match is approved, the winner gains points and the loser loses points.

3. Point Difference: The number of points exchanged depends on the rating difference between the two players:

  • If the higher-rated player wins (expected): Small point gain (1–9 points).
  • If the lower-rated player wins (upset): Large point gain (10–40 points).

4. ITTF Rating Table (simplified):

  • Rating diff 0–24: Winner +10, Loser −10
  • Rating diff 25–49: Winner +12, Loser −12
  • Rating diff 50–99: Winner +14, Loser −14
  • Rating diff 100–149: Winner +16, Loser −16
  • Rating diff 150–199: Winner +20, Loser −20
  • Rating diff 200–299: Winner +24, Loser −24
  • Rating diff 300–399: Winner +28, Loser −28
  • Rating diff 400–499: Winner +32, Loser −32
  • Rating diff 500–749: Winner +36, Loser −36
  • Rating diff 750+: Winner +40, Loser −40

5. Upset Bonus: If the lower-rated player wins, they gain the upset points shown above, and the higher-rated player loses the same amount.

6. Doubles: Points are calculated using the average rating of each pair.

7. No points are awarded until both players confirm the match result.
